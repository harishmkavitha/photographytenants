Behind the lens

Hi, I'm Meera

I started Halo & Grain in a one-room studio in Besant Nagar with a borrowed 50mm and a stubborn belief that the best photographs happen when nobody's performing for the camera.

Eleven years and a few hundred weddings later, that hasn't changed. We're a small team on purpose — the person you meet at your first coffee is the person shooting your day. We work in warm, honest light and hand you photographs that still feel true a decade from now.

Good to know

Frequently asked

How soon do we get our photos?

Sneak-peek edits land within 72 hours. The full edited gallery is ready in 4–6 weeks, and cinematic films follow 2–3 weeks after that. Rush delivery is available on request.

Do you travel? What about outstation charges?

Yes — we shoot across Tamil Nadu, Pondicherry, Bengaluru and destination weddings anywhere. Travel and stay are billed at actuals for events beyond 40 km from Chennai, agreed in writing before booking.

What deposit do you need to hold a date?

A 25% retainer confirms and blocks your date. The balance is split — a portion before the shoot and the remainder on final delivery.

Do we get the raw, unedited files?

We deliver a fully culled and colour-graded gallery rather than raw files — it's the work at its best. Raw files can be licensed separately if you specifically need them.

Is there a backup shooter and backup of our files?

Always. Every wedding is covered by at least two shooters, all cameras record to dual cards, and your files are backed up to three locations until final delivery.
